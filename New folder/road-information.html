Discover the Carpathians

The Transfăgărășan Road

The Transfăgărășan crosses the Făgăraș Mountains in central Romania and connects the regions of Muntenia and Transylvania. The spectacular mountain section is approximately 90 km long and reaches an altitude of around 2,042 metres near Bâlea Lake.

Road overview

What can you see?

The road links valleys, forests and high mountain terrain. Along the way, visitors can discover historic sites, impressive engineering and natural viewpoints.

  • Poenari Castle — A historic fortress associated with Vlad the Impaler.
  • Vidraru Dam — A huge dam surrounded by mountain scenery.
  • Bâlea Waterfall — One of the impressive natural attractions along the route.
  • Bâlea Lake — A beautiful glacial lake high in the mountains.
  • Făgăraș Mountains — Dramatic peaks and landscapes throughout the journey.
