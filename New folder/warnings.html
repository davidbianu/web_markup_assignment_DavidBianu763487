Discover the Carpathians

Stay Safe on the Mountain

The Transfăgărășan is a high-altitude mountain road where weather and road conditions can change quickly. Visitors should prepare before travelling and follow local warnings and restrictions.

🐻 Bears

Brown bears live in the Carpathian Mountains and may sometimes be seen near roads and tourist areas.

Remember:

  • Never approach or feed a bear.
  • Keep a safe distance.
  • Do not get out of your car to approach wildlife.
  • Do not leave food or rubbish outside.
  • Follow local wildlife instructions.

❄ Snow & Weather

Snow and ice can remain at higher elevations, and conditions can change quickly.

Remember:

  • Check current road conditions before travelling.
  • Check the weather forecast.
  • Follow road closure signs and barriers.
  • Drive carefully.
  • Be prepared for cold weather at high altitude.
⚠ Important warning: Never ignore road closures or safety warnings. Mountain conditions can change quickly.

Need assistance?

For emergencies in Romania, call 112. For road and weather updates, consult official local sources before travelling.
