The History of the Transfăgărășan

The Transfăgărășan was constructed between 1970 and 1974 during Romania's communist period. The road was commissioned under Nicolae Ceaușescu and was intended to provide a strategic route across the Carpathian Mountains.

Engineering through the mountains

A challenging construction project

Construction was extremely difficult because workers had to build through steep and rocky mountain terrain. The project required tunnels, bridges, viaducts and large amounts of rock excavation.

The road was officially opened in 1974 and later became one of Romania's most famous tourist attractions.

Key moments

  1. 1970 — Construction begins.
  2. 1970–1974 — Tunnels, bridges and mountain sections are constructed.
  3. 1974 — The Transfăgărășan officially opens.
  4. Today — The road is a popular tourist and driving destination.
